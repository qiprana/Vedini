About Viduranītiḥ

Viduranītiḥ is Vidura's counsel to Dhṛtarāṣṭra in the Udyoga Parva of the Mahābhārata. The supplied Samskrita Bharati recitation handout selects 25 ślokas. This study collection presents all 25 selected verses. Full-śloka and practice recordings are available for verses 1–18 and 20–25. Audio for verse 19 is still being prepared.
