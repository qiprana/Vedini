About Viduranītiḥ

Viduranītiḥ is Vidura's counsel to Dhṛtarāṣṭra in the Udyoga Parva of the Mahābhārata. The supplied Samskrita Bharati recitation handout selects 25 ślokas. This first study release contains verses 1–2.
